Licensing & citation

What’s open, what isn’t, and how to cite the work.

Written works (essays, whitepapers)

Essays, whitepapers, and other prose published on this site are licensed CC BY-SA 4.0. That permits sharing and adapting the work, including commercially, provided you give appropriate attribution and license any derivative under the same terms.

A reasonable attribution:

Higuera, D. (2026). [Essay title]. AES Research. https://aesresearch.ai/writing/[slug].html

Each essay carries its own citation block at the end — exact title, publication date, and a ready-made BibTeX entry. Use that block rather than reconstructing the citation from memory.

Models & training recipes

Models, fine-tuning recipes, and training pipelines referenced in the writing are not published. All rights reserved.

AI training

Content on this site may not be used to train AI or machine-learning models without written permission. CC BY-SA 4.0 covers human reuse with attribution — it does not extend to training or data-mining use. That reservation is expressed in machine-readable form at robots.txt, ai.txt, and .well-known/tdmrep.json.

How to cite

Citing a specific essay: use the citation block at the end of that essay — it carries the correct title, date, and BibTeX entry for that piece. Citing the site generally: AES Research, https://aesresearch.ai/.
